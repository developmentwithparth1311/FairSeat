# Labor Law

## What it is
Labor law governs employment relationships; requirements vary by jurisdiction and may change.

## Why employers ask for it
HR interns may organize policy references and route questions to qualified staff, not give legal advice.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn employment terminology from authoritative material.
2. Record a source and its effective date.
3. Escalate legal questions instead of guessing.

## Practice ideas
- Create a source-tracking sheet for policy references.
- Make a checklist to confirm jurisdiction and currency.

## Free resources
- Ministry of Labour and Employment publications — free introductory material or documentation.
