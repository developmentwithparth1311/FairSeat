# Analytics

## What it is
Analytics turns data into findings through clear questions, reliable data, and careful interpretation.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may clean spreadsheets, summarize trends, and present evidence to a team.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Practice spreadsheet sorting, filtering, and formulas.
2. Group data and compare time periods.
3. Explain one finding and its limitations.

## Practice ideas
- Analyze a small survey dataset.
- Build a one-page chart summary with three findings.
