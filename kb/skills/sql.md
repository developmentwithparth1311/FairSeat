# Sql

## What it is
SQL queries and manages relational data, including filtering, grouping, and combining tables.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may retrieve records, summarize results, and check that joins match the question.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, and aggregates.
2. Group records and join related tables.
3. Check row counts and sample results.

## Practice ideas
- Query a small bookstore database for sales trends.
- Join customer and order tables and explain results.
