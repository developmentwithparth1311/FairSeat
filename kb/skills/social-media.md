# Social Media

## What it is
Social media work plans and publishes content while listening to audience responses.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may prepare calendars, draft posts, and summarize engagement carefully.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn audience and content-calendar basics.
2. Write a post with one clear purpose.
3. Review sample engagement data and its limits.

## Practice ideas
- Build a one-week campus-club content calendar.
- Compare two sample posts and responses.
