# Payroll

## What it is
Payroll calculates and records employee pay and related deductions under applicable rules.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may check timesheets, organize inputs, and protect sensitive employee records.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn payroll terms and record accuracy basics.
2. Check a mock timesheet against a schedule.
3. Follow a review checklist and protect private data.

## Practice ideas
- Build a mock timesheet validation checklist.
- Reconcile sample hours with a fictional summary.
