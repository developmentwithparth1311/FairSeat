# Employee Relations

## What it is
Employee relations concerns the working relationship between an organization and its employees.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may organize policy information, prepare meeting notes, and route questions to HR staff.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn common HR terms and confidentiality basics.
2. Practice writing neutral, factual notes.
3. Study how organizations route workplace questions.

## Practice ideas
- Create an onboarding question guide.
- Map how a fictional employee query is routed.

## Free resources
- SHRM free HR articles and resources — free introductory material or documentation.
