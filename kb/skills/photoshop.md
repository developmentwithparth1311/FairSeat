# Photoshop

## What it is
Adobe Photoshop edits photographs and creates raster-based graphics.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may prepare image assets, make consistent edits, and export files for digital use.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn layers, selections, and non-destructive edits.
2. Practice cropping and adjusting an image.
3. Export versions sized for their intended use.

## Practice ideas
- Prepare consistent event images.
- Create a social post using original or licensed assets.
