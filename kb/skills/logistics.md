# Logistics

## What it is
Logistics plans movement and storage of goods, coordinating timing, capacity, and information.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may track shipments, update schedules, and help identify delays.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn transport and fulfillment terms.
2. Map a simple order-to-delivery process.
3. Track dates, status, and exceptions in a sheet.

## Practice ideas
- Map a delivery process and mark possible delays.
- Build a shipment tracker with expected dates.
