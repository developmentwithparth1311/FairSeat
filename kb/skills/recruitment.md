# Recruitment

## What it is
Recruitment attracts, reviews, and coordinates applicants for roles.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may schedule interviews, maintain status records, and explain next steps.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn common hiring stages and respectful communication.
2. Maintain a clear, structured tracker.
3. Handle candidate details through approved processes.

## Practice ideas
- Create an interview scheduling tracker.
- Draft a clear update for a fictional applicant.
