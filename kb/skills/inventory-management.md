# Inventory Management

## What it is
Inventory management tracks items an organization holds, uses, or sells.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may update stock records, compare counts, and flag unusual changes.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn stock, reorder, and lead-time terms.
2. Match item codes and quantities in a spreadsheet.
3. Compare recorded quantities with a mock count.

## Practice ideas
- Build a reorder tracker for products.
- Analyze a sample stock list for slow-moving items.
