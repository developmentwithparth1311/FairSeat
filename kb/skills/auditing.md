# Auditing

## What it is
Auditing is a structured review of records, processes, or controls against stated criteria.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may organize evidence, check sample transactions, and maintain clear review notes.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn the purpose of evidence, controls, and audit trails.
2. Trace a sample transaction from source to summary.
3. Record findings with dates and references.

## Practice ideas
- Create a checklist for a mock expense review.
- Trace sample transactions and document exceptions.
