# Branding

## What it is
Branding shapes how people recognize an organization through a consistent identity, voice, and promise.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may research audiences, organize assets, and check communications against guidelines.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Compare brands and describe their audiences.
2. Learn basic color, typography, and voice guidelines.
3. Create a one-page guide for a small project.

## Practice ideas
- Make a consistent set of event announcements.
- Review sample posts for consistent tone and visuals.
