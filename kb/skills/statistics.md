# Statistics

## What it is
Statistics describes data and helps reason about variation and uncertainty.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may summarize data, check sample sizes, and explain what results do and do not show.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Review averages, spread, and probability.
2. Plot data before choosing a summary.
3. Explain uncertainty and avoid causal overclaims.

## Practice ideas
- Summarize a survey with charts and caveats.
- Compare two groups and explain sample limits.
