# Marketing

## What it is
Marketing helps an organization understand audiences and communicate the value of its products or services.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may research audiences, organize campaign information, and summarize engagement.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn audience, positioning, and channel basics.
2. Connect a campaign goal to a measurable outcome.
3. Draft a short plan with a clear audience and message.

## Practice ideas
- Plan awareness for a campus event.
- Compare messages for two audience groups.
