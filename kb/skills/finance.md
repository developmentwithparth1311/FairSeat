# Finance

## What it is
Finance studies how people and organizations plan, obtain, and use money.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may prepare spreadsheet summaries, compare costs, and organize supporting information.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Review financial statements and key terms.
2. Calculate simple growth and percentage changes.
3. Explain assumptions alongside a spreadsheet analysis.

## Practice ideas
- Compare two sample budgets and identify trade-offs.
- Build a cash-flow forecast with stated assumptions.
