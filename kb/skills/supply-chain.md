# Supply Chain

## What it is
Supply chain management coordinates sourcing, production, inventory, and delivery.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may update trackers, analyze delays, and explain how stages affect one another.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Map the steps from supplier to customer.
2. Learn lead-time, demand, and inventory basics.
3. Find delays or stock risks in a small dataset.

## Practice ideas
- Map a fictional product's supply process.
- Analyze sample orders for late deliveries.
