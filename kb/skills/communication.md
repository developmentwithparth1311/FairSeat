# Communication

## What it is
Professional communication shares information clearly and respectfully through writing, speaking, and listening.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns use it to ask questions, summarize work, coordinate tasks, and explain findings.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Write the purpose of a message first.
2. Summarize a longer note in a few plain sentences.
3. Ask a peer to review a short update.

## Practice ideas
- Write a weekly project update with progress and blockers.
- Explain a chart to a non-specialist in two minutes.
