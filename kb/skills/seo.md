# Seo

## What it is
Search engine optimization helps useful web pages be discovered through search.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may research search terms, improve page titles, and review site content.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn how pages are discovered and indexed.
2. Write descriptive titles and headings.
3. Review a page for useful content and accessibility.

## Practice ideas
- Improve a fictional event page's structure.
- Check titles, headings, and image descriptions.

- Google Search Central documentation — free introductory material or documentation.
