# Figma

## What it is
Figma is a collaborative design tool for interface layouts and interactive prototypes.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may prepare screens, organize components, and share prototypes for feedback.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn frames, text, shapes, and alignment.
2. Recreate a simple mobile screen with components.
3. Link screens and collect feedback.

## Practice ideas
- Prototype a three-screen registration flow.
- Create reusable buttons and form fields.
