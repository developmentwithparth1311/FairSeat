# Accounting

## What it is
Accounting organizes and summarizes an organization's financial activity, including transactions and reports.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may classify expenses, check invoices, reconcile records, and help prepare routine statements.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn the accounting equation and basic statements.
2. Practice classifying sample transactions.
3. Reconcile a small ledger and explain differences.

## Practice ideas
- Create a monthly income and expense summary.
- Reconcile a sample ledger with a bank statement.
