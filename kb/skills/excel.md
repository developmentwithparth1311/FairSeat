# Excel

## What it is
Microsoft Excel organizes, calculates, and presents tabular information in spreadsheets.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may clean lists, use formulas, check totals, and prepare summaries.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Practice cell references, sorting, and filtering.
2. Learn SUM, IF, and lookup functions.
3. Build a pivot table and check its totals.

## Practice ideas
- Create a budget tracker with clear categories.
- Summarize a sample table with a pivot and chart.
