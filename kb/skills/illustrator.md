# Illustrator

## What it is
Adobe Illustrator is a vector graphics editor for artwork that scales without losing sharpness.

## Why employers ask for it
Interns may prepare icons, diagrams, and marketing artwork in reusable formats.

## How to get started (beginner path)
1. Learn paths, shapes, layers, and alignment.
2. Practice a small color and type system.
3. Export artwork for its intended use.

## Practice ideas
- Design a consistent set of event icons.
- Create an infographic from a short data table.
